What this app does

Victoria Off Road is an offline-first map app for recreational off-road navigation in Victoria, Australia. It shows map tiles and tracks on your device. It is not a commercial drone, aviation, or surveying product.

Information we collect

1. Location (GPS) — on your device only

  • The app may access your device location (precise GPS) so it can show your position on the map. It does not give turn-by-turn directions.
  • Location is processed on your device. We do not upload, store, or sell your live GPS track to our servers as part of normal map use.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission in Android Settings. Without location permission, map browsing still works; “my location” features will not.

2. Optional feedback (FormSubmit)

  • If you use the in-app Feedback form, you send a message and, optionally, your name and a reply email address.
  • That submission is sent over HTTPS via FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards it to our support inbox so we can read it and reply.
  • Do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information in feedback.
  • Victoria Off Road does not offer the “Suggest name / details” track form. It does not create an install ID and does not send track coordinates with feedback.

3. Google Play subscriptions and purchases

  • If you buy the optional Pro subscription through Google Play, payment is handled by Google. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • The app asks Google Play Billing on your device whether Pro is active. We do not send purchase details to a Trevs Shed server. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

4. Crash and diagnostics (Android / Google Play)

The Android system or Google Play may provide crash or performance reports to us. We use those only to fix bugs. The app has no separate crash-reporting SDK.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob) and the consent prompt (Google UMP)

  • The free app shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). Banner ads appear on the track list and track details screens. A rewarded ad, which you choose to watch, can unlock one offline map pack. There are no ads on the map or in Android Auto.
  • Where the law requires it (for example for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows a consent prompt before ads are loaded. You can review or change your choice at any time in Menu → Privacy & ad choices.
  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google: your device’s IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), app and ad interactions (such as app launches, taps and ad views), diagnostic information (such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use), and device identifiers (the Android advertising ID and app set ID). Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit.
  • We do not send your precise GPS position to AdMob.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings. Optional Pro removes ads.
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Map tiles

Basemap tiles are loaded over HTTPS from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). As with any web request, OpenFreeMap receives your device’s IP address. Downloaded map packs are kept on your device.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not send your live GPS position to advertisers or to us.
  • The app has no Trevs Shed account and no sign-in. You do not need an account to use any feature.
  • We do not include DEECA / ForestRoad track databases in this app.

Map data and attribution

  • Track lines and map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). The app attributes OpenStreetMap on the map and in About.
  • Basemap tiles: OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org), built from OpenStreetMap data.

Data security

Data sent by the app (feedback to FormSubmit, ad requests to Google, map tile requests to OpenFreeMap) is sent over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

  • We keep feedback emails only as long as needed to respond and improve the app, then delete them.
  • To ask us to delete a feedback message we still hold, write to PO Box 58, Mooroolbark VIC 3138, or use Menu → Feedback, and say which message to delete.
  • Your location is not stored by us off the device. Data Google holds for ads is managed through Google’s own tools and your Android advertising ID settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit a child’s personal information via Feedback.

Overseas disclosure

FormSubmit, Google (Play, AdMob, UMP) and OpenFreeMap may process data on servers outside Australia, under those providers’ terms.

Changes
