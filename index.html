What this app does

Victoria Off Road is an offline-first map app for recreational off-road navigation in Victoria, Australia. It shows map tiles and tracks on your device. It is not a commercial drone, aviation, or surveying product.

Information we collect

1. Account (for subscription)

  • To subscribe and unlock paid features you create an in-app account (typically email and password).
  • Account details are used only to sign you in and check subscription entitlement with Google Play.
  • We do not sell account information.

2. Location (GPS) — on your device only

  • The app may access your device location (precise GPS) so it can show your position on the map and help with navigation.
  • Location is processed on your device. We do not upload, store, or sell your live GPS track to our servers as part of normal map use.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission in Android Settings. Without location permission, map browsing still works; “my location” features will not.

3. Optional feedback (FormSubmit)

  • If you use the in-app Feedback form, you may choose to send a message and, optionally, your name and email address.
  • That submission is sent via FormSubmit to trevsshed4x4@gmail.com so we can respond.
  • Do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information in feedback.
  • FormSubmit’s own privacy practices apply to the transmission of that form. We keep feedback emails only as long as needed to respond and improve the app, then delete or archive them in the ordinary course of business.

4. Google Play / subscriptions and purchases

  • If you buy a subscription through Google Play, payment is handled by Google. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • Google may share with us limited purchase or subscription status information needed to unlock paid features (for example, that your account has an active entitlement). See Google Play’s privacy policy.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

5. Crash / diagnostics (if enabled by the store or OS)

  • The Android system or Google Play may provide anonymised crash or performance reports. We use those only to fix bugs. We do not use them to identify you personally.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use your location data for advertising.
  • We do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the paid build described by this policy.
  • We do not redistribute DEECA / ForestRoad proprietary track databases in this paid build.

Map data and attribution

Map display uses OpenStreetMap data and related open tiles (for example OpenFreeMap / OpenTopo-style layers).

  • OpenStreetMap data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  • Commercial use of OSM data is allowed under ODbL when attributed properly. This app attributes OSM on the map and/or in About.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit a child’s personal information via Feedback.

Overseas disclosure

Feedback email and Google Play processing may involve servers outside Australia (for example FormSubmit or Google). By using those features you understand that limited data may be handled overseas under those providers’ terms.

Your choices

  • Revoke location permission in system settings at any time.
  • Do not use Feedback if you do not want to send name/email.
  • Cancel your subscription in Google Play at any time.
  • Contact us to request deletion of a feedback email we still hold: trevsshed4x4@gmail.com.
